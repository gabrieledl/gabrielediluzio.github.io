About

I am a PhD Candidate in Finance and Institutions at the Department of Social and Economic Sciences of Sapienza University of Rome. During my PhD, I have been a visiting student at the Department of Statistics of Ludwig Maximilian University of Munich and the Munich Center for Machine Learning.

I focus on modelling dependence among financial variables in both cross-sectional and time-varying settings.

My doctoral thesis, Essays on Covariance Matrix Estimation Using Copula Functions, is scheduled for submission on 31 October 2026.
